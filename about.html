About Us

Our Purpose

Madison Private Equity Collective brings together James Madison University students who want to learn more about private equity, investing, and business. Through discussions, workshops, and connections with peers and professionals, our club aims to help members build their knowledge and explore future career opportunities. Whether you are just getting started or already have an interest in finance, we hope to provide a welcoming place to ask questions, share ideas, and grow together.

Assisting students at James Madison University with navigating a future in Private Equity. Based in Harrisonburg, VA
